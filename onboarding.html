Onboarding

Welcome to the Hack The Box Meetup Zurich! Enter your email address and the meetup code from the intro slide to get access to tonight's lab on the Hack The Box Enterprise platform. This works whether this is your first meetup or not.

If you've joined before, use the email address of your existing Hack The Box Enterprise account.
Shown at the start of the meetup.

What happens next

  1. First time here? You'll get an invitation email from Hack The Box. Follow the link to create your account. The Meetup Lab is included.
  2. Joined before? Sign in to enterprise.hackthebox.com with your existing account. The Meetup Lab has been added to it.
  3. Open the Meetup Lab. It has the violet icon in the left navigation pane.

Access lasts a few days after the meetup. Something not working? Grab the organiser.
